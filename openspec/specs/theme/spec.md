# Theme

## Purpose
Define the accepted presentation contract for Fiestaaa.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Shared surfaces and controls
All screens SHALL use Manrope, semantic theme colors and common spacing, cards, buttons, fields and headers. Pages SHALL be at most 1200px wide, forms 760px, with the navigation rail from 720px. QR black-on-white and camera contrast SHALL remain intentional exceptions. Public HTML SHALL follow the theme without changing legal text.

#### Scenario: Large text on mobile
- **WHEN** an interface uses 200% text at 360px
- **THEN** actions stack or wrap without truncating essential labels
